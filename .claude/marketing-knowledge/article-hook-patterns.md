## Hook formulas to consider

The updev/UniPixel canonical version of this doc catalogues 8 patterns:

1. **I-statement question** — "How do I set up Meta CAPI?"-shape hooks that meet a literal search query head-on
2. **vs / alternatives** — "X vs Y" — comparison-driven, captures buyers in evaluation
3. **Watch-out warning** — "Here's what nobody tells you about..." — confidential-feeling, builds trust
4. **Hidden-cost gotcha** — surfaces a cost or consequence the reader hadn't considered
5. **Universal fear** — "Your X is broken" — broad-appeal awareness piece
6. **Category capture** — "The complete guide to..." — SEO-led pillar content
7. **X without Y** — "Server-side tracking without GTM" — names the constraint that makes the offer notable
8. **How-to** — direct instructional content
